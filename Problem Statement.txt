Problem Statement
Financial Analytics and Learning Dashboard
Develop a web-based Financial Analytics and Learning Dashboard that provides role-based access and management capabilities for Workers, Managers, Company Owners, and Labor Department Officers.
The system should support financial tracking, employee payment management, complaint handling, and analytical reporting through dedicated dashboards and portals.
________________________________________
User Roles
1. Worker
•	Register and login to the system.
•	View personal profile.
•	View current salary.
•	View salary increment history.
•	View credited and debited amounts.
•	View overpaid amounts.
•	View shortfall amounts.
•	Raise complaints or cases.
•	Track complaint status and resolutions.
•	Access learning dashboard and assigned learning content.
2. Manager
•	Login through a dedicated manager portal.
•	View all workers under management.
•	Monitor worker payments.
•	Track salary increments.
•	View overpayments and shortfalls.
•	Review worker complaints.
•	Generate financial summaries and reports.
3. Company Owner
•	Login through a dedicated owner portal.
•	View company-wide financial analytics.
•	Monitor all credits and debits.
•	Review salary expenditures.
•	View equipment purchase expenses.
•	Analyze operational costs.
•	Access high-level financial dashboards and reports.
4. Labor Department Officer
•	Login through a dedicated labor department portal.
•	View employee complaints and case details.
•	Track complaint investigations.
•	Resolve, reject, or close cases.
•	Maintain complaint resolution history.
